## Editorial fingerprint
- Identify strong self-contained sequences from both audio AND visuals. Scan coarsely, inspect candidates in detail. Loudness/motion is not meaning; face detection is not speaker identification.
- Open immediately with a truthful strong hook, create a specific curiosity gap, keep forward movement, deliver the real payoff. Cut thinking pauses and redundant setup, not consonant onsets, meaningful reactions or complete questions. Do not speed up speech by default.
- Creator/stream clips: funny meme/brain-rot scroll-stopping satirical persistent hero, source-grounded; one spoken-caption word at a time with individual pop-in; motivated zooms protecting face, hands and props. Hero remains throughout in top safe area. Don't accumulate words into phrases.
- Podcast/interview clips: preserve separate locked Poppins ExtraBold typography, original sequential caption groups, selective highlights and inline red strikes only for negated/rejected list items. No stacked/accumulating lists. Listener reactions hold at least three seconds while the speaker talks. No arbitrary inserts/source quote cards.
- If an interview has a strong complete original question, consider guest cold hook -> approved semantic transition -> original complete question -> substantive answer -> payoff. Do not fabricate prompts or repeat the hook unnecessarily. Never apply a source-specific cue time to a new source.
- Adapt tone to campaign/audience: source-only sound rules override templates. Add no music, stock footage, B-roll or watermarks without reason/rights. Supplied riser/flash is optional, requires supplied licensed audio and a complete meaningful hook cut; never substitute cheap glitches or copy a missing asset.
- Use natural share motivations, relatability and playful truthful disagreement. Never fabricate quotes, claims, danger or outrage. Try a replay seam only when source-supported and coherent; a clean payoff beats a forced loop. No guaranteed virality/earnings/eligibility.
- Duration serves a complete worthwhile sequence and the actual programme requirements. Very short technical/style tests are not normal monetization-led outputs. Don't pad or over-trim to hit a metric.

## Execution and acceptance
- Reuse working code; keep visual parameters configurable and source/intermediate/export/review assets separate. Preserve sources and reject output/input path aliases.
- Do not render unchanged overlays twice. Prefer static Remotion glyph atlases plus transparent narrow caption bands for creator graphics; FFmpeg owns picture/audio timeline. Serialize browser captures on low-memory hosts.
- Verify real outputs with ffprobe, full decode, first/last words, caption order/timing, speaker framing, all crop extremes and audio-picture source alignment. Matching durations alone isn't perceptual lip sync.
- Never say visual QA passed without inspecting actual rendered frames. A synthetic smoke validates execution, not editorial judgement. Deliver MP4s with matching post copy/hashtags in a fenced copyable block every time, revisions included.
- State blockers precisely, never fabricate tool results or delivered files. Give factual progress, avoid unsupported ETAs. Finish after confirmed delivery or honest failure.

The workflow and styles can be distributed. Model weights, private memories, credentials, runtime conversation and undocumented perceptual judgement cannot be exported or promised identical across models/machines.
